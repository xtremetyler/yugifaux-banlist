The short version

How YugiFaux Works

Create a custom archetype, improve it through matches, and eventually retire it into the shared format. This page is a plain-language guide; League Admins resolve unusual cases.

01

Deck construction

Build around your active archetype

  • Follow the current Dynamic Limeade List copy limits.
  • Play at least 1 copy of every card in your active archetype, up to 3 copies.
  • Your active archetype must appear at least 5 cards more than any other single archetype or theme across your Main, Extra, and Side Decks.
02

Custom creation

Create one cohesive archetype

  • Create at least 12 cards, including 2 Main Deck card types and at least 1 Extra Deck card.
  • You begin with no more than 45 total effect clauses across the archetype.
  • Cards must share names, referenced cards, motifs, mechanics, or direct strategic support—not merely a generic word.
  • Before activation, every final card must be posted and counted by 2 players, including a League Admin.
  • Cards must use appropriate Problem-Solving Card Text and cannot be hateful, malicious, NSFW, consistently unreactable, strictly superior to restricted TCG cards, or overloaded with disconnected floodgates.
What does not count as an effect clause?

Names, original stats, Level/Rank/Link Rating, Attribute, type, ability, materials, timing specifications, explicit costs, limiters, and hindrances generally do not count. Triggers, payoffs, activated choices, numerical changes, and alternate name/type/archetype treatments generally do.

03

League play

Play best-of-three matches

  • Sideboarding is allowed between games.
  • Earn 2 points for every game you win. A player who loses a full match 0–2 still earns 1 point.
  • You buff your opponent’s active archetype by the number of effect clauses equal to the points earned from your game wins.
  • Apply and post all buffs before playing another sanctioned match.
  • Buffs cannot change a card’s fundamental card type, monster type, Attribute, Level, materials, or Extra Deck method. A monster may become a Pendulum Monster if its scales are agreed upon.
  • If you lose 2–1 against an archetype that began at net +12 clauses or higher, its player receives the would-be buff amount as points instead.
04

End of a reign

Retire into one of three destinations

Ephtear

Lose a match after beginning at net +12 clauses. The creator normally earns 5 points and may override one retired card to Un-Limeade.

Duelhalla

Survive while 4 other archetypes retire to Ephtear. The creator normally earns 5 points and may override one retired card to Banned. Duelhalla retirements do not advance other archetypes’ retirement counts.

Limb-Faux

An archetype found inappropriate is forcibly retired and all its cards are banned. Its creator earns no retirement points; every other player earns 5 and the creator incurs Double Garnet Tax.

After retirement, the remaining cards enter the shared pool. Other players vote on each card’s Limeade status; majority wins and ties use the lower copy limit.

05

Point shop

Spend points to shape the League

5

Clause buffer

Add a one-time 4-clause buffer before an Ephtear retirement.

6+

Wild card

Commission a random player to make a 3-clause Wild card; spend 2 per extra clause, up to 3 times.

7

Official card

Add a non-banned Official TCG card from an eligible category to the shared pool.

9

Custom card

Add a non-archetypal custom card with up to 4 effect clauses.

10

Limeade shift

Adjust 3 differently named, non-banned cards while moving at least one higher and one lower.

12

Revive

Claim another creator’s Ephtear archetype once, then add 4 cards using up to 15 clauses.

15

Commission an archetype

Randomly assign another player to create your next 12-card archetype; they earn 5 points.

New-player card pool allowance

On joining, a player may add previously unused Official TCG cards at Semi-Limeade: 3 Main Deck monsters, 2 Extra Deck monsters, 3 Spells, 2 Traps, and 1 hand trap. They may also add one non-archetypal custom card with up to 2 clauses. Dedicated archetype additions require unanimous approval after the first card from that archetype enters the pool.

Late-join assistance

A player joining after at least 3 archetypes have retired may choose up to 2 point-shop options at a personal cost of 0 points, subject to League administration.
