A format built by its players

Every duel writes the next chapter

Your active archetype is never finished. Match results award points and let opponents add new effect clauses, creating a format that develops through play instead of remaining static.

01

Create

Design a cohesive custom archetype with at least 12 cards, multiple Main Deck card types, and an Extra Deck card.

02

Compete

Play best-of-three matches using your active archetype and the evolving shared Limeade card pool.

03

Evolve

Earn points from games and receive effect-clause buffs that must be applied before your next League match.

04

Retire

Send an archetype to Ephtear, Duelhalla, or Limb-Faux and let its cards permanently change the format.
